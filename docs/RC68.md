# Relay Lab RC68

Build 104, Android 12 or newer, ARM64.

- The Apps desktop uses a themed long-press menu and moves each icon with its label during a drag.
- Swipeable Apps pages use dots and edge arrows. Hold an app over another to group them. Folders can be named, renamed, and dissolved, and apps can be dragged out.
- Settings uses two columns on compact phones and adapts to very narrow screens and large system text.

Verification completed for 192 automated tests across 35 suites, debug build, signed preview build, preview lint, and APK packaging. Physical phone testing remains pending.
